TechStore Warranty Policy

Selected TechStore products include a limited 1-year warranty from the date of purchase.

The warranty covers manufacturing defects that occur during normal use.

The warranty does not normally cover accidental damage, liquid damage, unauthorized modifications, or normal cosmetic wear.

Customers should provide proof of purchase when requesting warranty service.

If a product is determined to have a covered manufacturing defect, TechStore may repair or replace the product depending on the circumstances.

Warranty claims should be submitted through TechStore customer support.

The warranty period begins on the original purchase date.

Products purchased from unauthorized sellers may not be covered by the TechStore warranty.
