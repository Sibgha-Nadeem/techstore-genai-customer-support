TechStore Return Policy

TechStore accepts returns within 30 days of the purchase date.

To be eligible for a return, the product must be in its original condition and include the original packaging and accessories.

Products that have been physically damaged by the customer may not be eligible for a return.

Customers should contact TechStore customer support before sending a product back.

Once a returned product is received, TechStore will inspect it before approving the return.

Approved refunds are issued using the original payment method.

Refund processing may take 5 to 7 business days after the return has been approved.

Digital products and software licenses are not eligible for returns.

If a product arrives damaged or defective, the customer should contact support as soon as possible.
