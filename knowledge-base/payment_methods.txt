TechStore Payment Methods

TechStore accepts Visa and Mastercard debit or credit cards.

TechStore also accepts PayPal for eligible orders.

Customers must provide accurate billing information when placing an order.

Payment is normally processed when an order is placed.

If a payment fails, the customer should verify their payment details and try again.

TechStore does not store customers' complete card numbers in its customer support system.

Refunds are normally sent back to the original payment method used for the purchase.

Customers should contact their bank or payment provider if a completed refund does not appear within the expected processing period.
