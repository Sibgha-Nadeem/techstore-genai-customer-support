TechStore Shipping Policy

TechStore currently offers standard and express shipping.

Standard shipping usually takes 3 to 5 business days.

Express shipping usually takes 1 to 2 business days.

Shipping times begin after the order has been processed.

Orders are processed within 1 business day.

Customers receive a tracking number after their order has been shipped.

Shipping times may be longer during weekends, public holidays, or periods of unusually high order volume.

Customers should make sure their shipping address is correct before placing an order.

TechStore currently delivers to addresses within its supported delivery regions.

Customers can contact support if their tracking information has not been updated for several business days.
